Why So Few Guesses?

Every guess moves one level down a tree. The number of guesses is the depth of the tree.

Depth of the tree

1 to 100: halve 100 → 50 → 25 → 12 → 6 → 3 → 1. That is 7 levels, so at most 7 guesses.

In plain English

How many times can you halve N until 1 is left? That count is log2(N), the depth of the tree.

1 to 1,000,000: 20 levels, so at most 20 guesses.
220 = 1,048,576 ≥ 1,000,000

Takeaways
StrategyGuess the middle, throw away half, repeat.
RequirementThe data must be sorted.
SpeedAbout log2(N) guesses: 7 for 100, 20 for a million, 30 for a billion.
OptimalWith higher or lower answers, no strategy can do better.

Where you will see it: looking up a word in a dictionary, git bisect for finding a bug, database indexes.
