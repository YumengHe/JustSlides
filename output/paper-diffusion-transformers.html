Step 2: How Should t and c Enter the Network?

In-context conditioning

Append the embeddings of t and c as two extra tokens, like the cls token in ViT, and drop them at the output. Almost no extra Gflops.

Cross-attention

Add a cross-attention layer after self-attention in every block; image tokens attend to the (t, c) embeddings. About 15% more Gflops.

Adaptive LayerNorm (adaLN)

Regress the LayerNorm scale γ and shift β from the sum of the t and c embeddings instead of learning them. Adds the least compute of all four.

adaLN-Zero

adaLN plus a scale α applied to each residual branch, also regressed from t + c, with the regressor zero-initialised so α = 0 at the start of training.

adaLN-Zero gives the lowest FID at every model size, for almost no compute. It is the one real architectural novelty in the paper.

Peebles and Xie, 2023, Sec. 3.2 and Fig. 5 (FID-50K over training for the four variants on DiT-XL/2).

Discussion: Did the Transformer Take Over?

Sora and video

OpenAI's Sora (2024) is a diffusion transformer over spacetime patches of a video latent; its technical report cites DiT as the backbone it scales.

Stable Diffusion 3 and Flux

SD3 (2024) introduced MMDiT, a two-stream DiT in which text and image tokens have separate weights but attend jointly. Flux (2024) and later models kept the design.

Follow-ups on the same backbone

SiT (2024) swaps DDPM for flow matching on an unchanged DiT and improves FID. REPA (2024) aligns DiT features with DINOv2 and trains many times faster.

Remaining weaknesses

Only class-conditional ImageNet; depends on a frozen Stable Diffusion VAE; attention is quadratic in tokens, so patch 2 is costly at high resolution; FID is the only metric.

Within two years the diffusion transformer became the default backbone for image and video generation.

Sources: OpenAI Sora technical report (2024); Esser et al., SD3 (ICML 2024); Black Forest Labs, Flux (2024); Ma et al., SiT (ECCV 2024); Yu et al., REPA (ICLR 2025).

Questions for Discussion

1.  Was the U-Net's inductive bias ever necessary, or did data and compute simply grow past the point where it mattered?

2.  adaLN handles one class label. What should conditioning look like for a 77-token text prompt, and is MMDiT the right answer?

3.  Patch 2 costs about 16× patch 8. Is quadratic attention over latent tokens sustainable at 1024 × 1024 or for a minute of video?

4.  Quality tracked Gflops almost perfectly here. Where should the next 10× go: depth, width, more tokens or more sampling steps?

5.  The paper reports FID only. Would the ranking of U-Net versus DiT change under human preference or text-alignment metrics?

ArchitectureReplace the U-Net with a ViT over latent patches; inject t and c with adaLN-Zero.
ScalingFID tracks forward-pass Gflops (correlation −0.93); patch size is a compute dial independent of parameters.
ResultDiT-XL/2 reaches FID 2.27 on ImageNet 256 × 256, state of the art, at about 6× fewer Gflops than ADM.
LegacySora, Stable Diffusion 3 and Flux are all diffusion transformers.
