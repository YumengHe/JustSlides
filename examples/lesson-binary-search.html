What Strategy Did I Use?

Every turn, the same three steps

  1. 1Guess the middle of what is left.
  2. 2Listen: higher or lower?
  3. 3Throw away the half that cannot contain the answer.

Repeat until found.

This strategy is called binary search.

A dictionary has 1,000 pages in alphabetical order. Using binary search, how many page checks do you need, at most, to find one word?

Question 2

Your TA drops a messy, unsorted pile of 200 exams on the desk. Can you use binary search to find your exam?

No. The pile is not sorted. Sort it first, or check one by one.

Takeaways
StrategyGuess the middle, throw away half, repeat.
RequirementThe data must be sorted.
SpeedAbout log2(N) guesses: 7 for 100, 20 for a million, 30 for a billion.
OptimalWith higher or lower answers, no strategy can do better.

Where you will see it: looking up a word in a dictionary, git bisect for finding a bug, database indexes.
